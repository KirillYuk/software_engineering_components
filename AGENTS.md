# AGENTS.md — Working Rules

1. **Role:** Complete lab assignments independently according to the requirements. The user reviews the results, identifies discrepancies, and provides prompts for corrections.

2. **Language:** Communicate and explain in Ukrainian. Use English for code, identifiers, and technical terms unless the assignment requires otherwise.

3. **Sources of requirements:** Priority order: official assignment, instructor's grading criteria and DoD, repository rules, and previous labs.

4. **Working with the repository:** Before starting, check the relevant files, instructions, structure, and Git status. Consider related artifacts regardless of their location. Do not re-analyze what is already known.

5. **Changes:** Create and edit the necessary files without asking for approval for each change. Ask for clarification only when there is significant ambiguity or a risk of going beyond the task's scope.

6. **Git safety:** Do not perform destructive operations that could cause data loss or change Git history without separate approval.

7. **Commits:** Commits, pushes, merges, pull requests, and publishing require separate approval. Use Conventional Commits. Record verified stages and fixes in separate commits, as required by the course.

8. **Quality:** Do not invent requirements, sources, errors, or verification results. Check consistency across models, specifications, and other artifacts, including previous labs. Before fixing discrepancies, determine whether `spec.md` or the acceptance criteria need to be updated.

9. **Reporting:** After each logical stage, briefly report what was done, verification results, problems, and the next step. Avoid unnecessary repetition.

10. **Lab requirements and AI trace:** Follow local instructions and the DoD. For AI-assisted work, keep a complete record of prompts and responses in the `ai/` directory. Help the user document AI errors they have verified and their corrections. Link each discrepancy to the relevant prompt, feedback, and additional acceptance criteria in `spec.md`. Do not invent errors or interaction history.

11. **Defense:** Prepare defense materials according to the course requirements. Do not invent decisions, checks, or evidence. The user reviews the materials and must understand and be able to explain the decisions independently.
